# Global leaders in restaurant management / POS — feature & pricing benchmark (2025–2026)

Scope: Toast, Square for Restaurants, Lightspeed Restaurant (K-Series), TouchBistro, Clover, Oracle MICROS Simphony, Revel Systems, Loyverse, Odoo POS Restaurant. Research date: 2026-11-10. Prices are USD unless stated. Official pricing pages for Toast and Oracle returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so those vendors' figures rely on 2026 third-party pricing guides and are flagged as such.

## KQ1 — What is the complete module list a top-tier platform offers, and which modules are table-stakes vs premium?

### Takeaway
By 2026 every leader ships the same core (menu/modifiers, order taking, floor plan, kitchen printing/KDS, integrated payments with bill splitting, basic reporting, staff roles, offline order-taking); the differentiation and the money are in add-on layers — online ordering/QR, delivery connectors, recipe-level inventory & profit management, loyalty/marketing/gift cards, payroll, reservations, kiosks, multi-location hierarchy, API access and AI assistants — which are sold as separate monthly fees almost everywhere except Loyverse (freemium) and Odoo (per-user ERP bundle).

## KQ2 — How do they price (monthly SaaS, per-location, per-terminal, processing %), with concrete figures?

### Takeaway
Three pricing archetypes dominate: (1) low/zero software fee subsidized by mandatory in-house payment processing at 2.3–3.1% + 10–15¢ (Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed, Revel Advantage); (2) quote-only enterprise metering (Oracle Simphony per revenue center + per transaction, multi-year contracts); (3) processing-agnostic subscriptions — Loyverse's $5–$25 add-ons and Odoo's $24.90–$49 per user per month. Contract lock-in (24–36 months with early-termination fees) is the norm for Toast, Revel and Clover bundles; Square and Loyverse are month-to-month.

### Inferences
- Effective monthly software cost for a single full-service location with floor plan + KDS + online ordering + loyalty: Toast ≈ $69 + ~$25 + $75 + $50 = ~$219 before processing; Square Premium ≈ $149 + $20/KDS device; Lightspeed Starter ≈ $69 + $30/KDS screen (online ordering included); TouchBistro ≈ $69 + $19 + $50 + $99 = ~$237; Clover ≈ $89.95 (KDS hardware extra); Loyverse ≈ $0–$35; Odoo ≈ $24.90 × users (POS app alone is free).
- Processing is the real revenue engine: at 2.49–2.6% + 15¢, a restaurant doing $800K/yr pays ~$20K+/yr in fees — an order of magnitude more than software fees — which is why Toast/Square/Clover/Revel bundle or mandate processing.
- Odoo's per-user model is unusual for POS; it's cheap for a single owner-operator (one POS user) but scales with back-office users, and Odoo's country-specific price tiers make it materially cheaper in some markets than the US list.

## KQ3 — Which platforms offer true offline operation, and how?

### Takeaway
All nine claim offline operation, but only three architectures keep multi-terminal service fully coherent without internet: on-premises servers (TouchBistro's local Mac/iPad server; Oracle Simphony's CAPS service), device-level caching with LAN peer sync (Lightspeed, Odoo), and device-isolated queues (Toast: orders stay on the device that took them). Card payments offline are the weak point everywhere — stored-and-forwarded with merchant liability and time caps (Square 24 h, Clover 7 days) or simply unavailable (Lightspeed).
