# Algerian market — local competitors, delivery, payments, fiscal rules (researched 2026-11-11)

## Local restaurant/POS competitors (prices in DZD)
- **Almawarid POS / Logiciel restaurant** — floor plan, kitchen dispatch, tactile POS with split billing (cash+card on one table), ingredient stock auto-depletion, payroll & HR (CNAS/IRG), multi-user with per-server drawer; DGI conformity (D.E. 05-468), TVA 19%/9%, NIF/NIS/RC/AI; data hosted in Algeria (loi 18-07); French + Arabic; web + Android + Windows, "no special hardware". Pricing "sur demande" (two tiers: Standard ≤5 employees, Enterprise manager+25); third-party comparison cites free → ~20,000 DA/year. **No website, no online ordering, no delivery integration, no multi-branch mentioned.** — https://almawarid.app/logiciel-restaurant-algerie/ ; https://zoomalgerie.com/logiciel-caisse-pos-algerie/
- **Restorium (Cirtasoft, Constantine)** — real-time orders, stock & preparations, "KitApp" kitchen display, mobile ordering, dashboards, temperature monitoring & raw-material traceability; targets restaurants, fast-food, sandwich shops, cafeterias; custom quote by modules/size (a comparison cites ~25,000 DZD). — https://www.cirtasoft.com/logiciel-restaurant/en/ ; https://www.cirtait.com/secteurs/restaurant/ ; https://makeitnow.fr/restorium-retour-dexperience-dun-restaurateur-apres-plusieurs-mois-dusage
- **Symloop POS** — Arabic + French; Business **65,000 DA/year** (up to 3 POS stations, stock, tables, loyalty, advanced reports, priority support); Premium **120,000 DA/year** (unlimited stations, **multi-location**, **KDS**, API integrations, custom reports, 24/7 support). Symloop also sells restaurant websites / mobile apps / delivery management / digital marketing. — https://symloop.com/blog/logiciel-caisse-pos-restaurant-commerce-setif-2026/ ; https://symloop.com/fr/blog/site-web-restaurant-algerie-2026/
- **STOCK-DZpro** — 2,500–8,900 DA/month; commerce/superette oriented (POS, stock, clients, lots, multi-dépôts, API, local mode). — zoomalgerie comparatif
- **Costy Stock** — free → 149 €/month; fast-food/pizzeria recipe cards (fiches techniques), ingredient destocking, food-cost. — zoomalgerie comparatif ; https://costy.app/
- **INNOVA POS** — custom quote; tactile, peripherals/scales, restaurant module. — zoomalgerie comparatif
- Also present: Homtaf (free POS+KDS panel) https://homtaf.com/panel/caisse-pos-kds-restaurant-boutique ; FloPOS "free open source for Algeria" https://flopos.com/restaurant-pos-algeria ; Gemba Food https://gemba-food.com/

## What Algerian buyers are told to prioritise (zoomalgerie comparatif)
1. Real offline capability ("coupez réellement Internet" during the demo). 2. Data portability (full export of products/clients/sales/stock). 3. Workflow fit (restaurants need tables + ingredient tracking). 4. 3-year total cost incl. hardware, maintenance, multi-site. 5. Local compliance — **no general obligation of certified POS software exists in official sources**, but invoicing rules per activity/regime must be respected.

## Delivery / online ordering ecosystem
- **Yassir** (Algerian super-app: rides, food, grocery, payments) is the main food-delivery platform; restaurants get a Partner app (YASSIR FOOD Partner on App Store), onboarding = profile config + training + menu upload; commissions reported at **15–30% per order**; integrations exist that push orders automatically into POS (Glovo/Kool/Yassir connectors mentioned by integrators). — https://yassir.com/fr/restaurant ; https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/yassir-food-partner/id1470937505 ; https://symloop.com/fr/blog/site-web-restaurant-algerie-2026/
- **Jumia exited Algeria in February 2026** (so Jumia Food is gone). — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jumia
- Local delivery apps also appear (fastapp.dz "Fast" in Algiers). — https://fastapp.dz/livraison/alger

## Payments
- **CIB card** (banks) — accepted on all SATIM-approved TPE and e-commerce; most widespread. **Edahabia** (Algérie Poste) — the reference card for domestic online payment, works on TPE at merchants incl. restaurants. **BaridiMob** — since **14 June 2025** merchants can be paid via **Baridi Pay** (QR/mobile). **SATIM** — national switch behind every gateway. — https://webminds.dz/blog/paiement-en-ligne-algerie-cib-dahabia.html ; https://www.centeem.com/en/blog/paiement-mobile-algerie-2026 ; https://dzbuild.com/blog/accept-online-payment-algeria

## Fiscal / legal (2025–2026)
- Consumer sales need a **cash-register ticket** (or other proof); invoice on request. Paper invoices still accepted; DGI moving to **e-invoicing in phases**. No official fiscal-device/real-time reporting obligation identified. — https://dddinvoices.com/learn/fiscalization-in-algeria-2026-pos-reporting-rules ; https://lamacta.com/en/blog/guide-facturation-legale-algerie-2026/
- **Timbre fiscal** on receipts/tickets paid in cash (LF2025 progressive rate per 100 DA bracket). — ibid.
- **TVA 9%** reduced rate extended to classified tourist restaurant services until **31 Dec 2027**; standard 19%. — ibid.
- **LF2026 art. 74 → art. 20 ter CPF**: on tax audit, taxpayers recording sales with software/cash systems must present an **editor's engagement (attestation) or a certificate from an accredited body** that the software ensures **immutability, security, conservation and archiving** of data; a third-party summary cites a 500,000 DA sanction per software (art. 115 bis) from 1 Jan 2027 — verify in the JO text. — https://www.mf.gov.dz/images/pdf/loidefinance/Loi_de_Finances_2026_FR.pdf ; https://legal-doctrine.com/en/edition/le-projet-de-loi-de-finances-2026-en-algerie-5d9451e67a4ec331039a1cb9ceeacd12 ; https://www.upgrowth.dz/journal-officiel/loi-finances-2026-obligations-declaratives-entreprises
